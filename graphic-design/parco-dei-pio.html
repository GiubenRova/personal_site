Parco dei Pio

From Ruben Giovannoni's personal archive

A 2020 visual-identity proposal for a new park planned for Santa Croce, near Carpi (Modena), and an early exploration of 3D rendering.

Contents
  1. Background
  2. Development
  3. Outcome

Background

In the first quarter of 2020, a local authority invited several firms to propose a visual identity for a new park in Santa Croce, a suburban area of Carpi. The project became an opportunity to learn and apply 3D-rendering techniques in a real design context.

Development

The work started with multiple logo directions. Each proposed identity was then extended into merchandise and environmental applications. The next step was creating 3D visualisations of signage intended for the physical park.

Outcome

The proposal was not selected, but it became a useful early experiment in translating a graphic identity into three-dimensional, spatial applications.

Prototype note: the final version can include the original project images here as a gallery.
