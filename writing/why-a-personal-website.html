Why keep a personal website?

09 October 2026

A personal site can be smaller than a portfolio and more permanent than a social profile. It can simply point to the places where the real work lives.

The appeal is not that everything has to be published here. The appeal is that this address remains the stable entry point: a name, a short description, a directory, and a few notes that belong nowhere else.

A root, not a container

This site does not need to absorb every project. It only needs to connect them. That makes it easier to keep small, useful and personal.
